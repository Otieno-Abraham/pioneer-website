Compliance

DNC is handled before, during and after every dial

01

Before import

Every list is checked against our master internal opt-out and DNC database.

02

In the dialer

VICIdial and Dial Africa automatically block any number flagged DNC or previously opted out.

03

On the live call

A homeowner asks to be removed? The agent tags the call DNC and the number is suppressed from all future dials.
