The dialer, the seats and the pricing, in plain terms

What runs the calls, what you pay for, what we need from you, and how the two commercial models work.

Do you pay for dialer seats?

Short answer: no. There is no per-seat or per-user dialer fee.

VICIdial is our own dialer. We run it on our own servers, so there is no licence to buy and no software seat to pay for. RevScalers does not set up, host or subscribe to anything.

A seat is one agent on the phones. Adding a seat means adding an agent. You pay for that agent’s hours (hourly model) or we earn on their leads (revenue share). Nothing extra is added for the dialer itself.

The dialer cost, call recording, supervision and QA are covered inside the hourly rate.

What you do not pay

  • Dialer licence or per-user fee
  • Server or hosting
  • Call recording storage
  • Setup of lists, DNC blocking or reports

What you do pay

  • Hourly model: the agreed rate per agent hour
  • Revenue share: nothing up front, we are paid a share of each sold lead
  • Any extra agent you ask us to add (same rate, no new fee)

Hourly example

5 agents × 8 hours × 5 days = 200 hours a week. At $5 per hour that is at most $1,000 a week, whatever the lead volume.

Seats scale the same way: 10 agents on the same schedule is 400 hours, at most $2,000 a week. No other charges.

Revenue share example

A client pays $100 for a lead. Pioneer receives $50. If the lead does not sell, nothing is paid.

Blended option

A lower hourly rate to cover our base costs, plus a smaller share of each sold lead. You get a lower fixed cost and we stay rewarded for quality.

Why we recommend hourly when we do the data work

If we source the lists, run the dialer and carry the agent cost, a commission-only model puts all of that cost on us before a single lead has sold. If RevScalers supplies the data, the biggest cost is gone and the revenue share becomes much easier for us to accept.
