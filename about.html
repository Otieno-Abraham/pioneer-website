OUR STORY

From Nairobi to the World

Building Kenya’s most trusted BPO company

Pioneer Outsourcing BPO started in Nairobi with a simple goal: deliver outsourcing work that holds up to Western standards, while building good jobs in East Africa. What began as a small team has grown into a BPO company serving clients across the USA, UK, and Europe, under the leadership of Chief of Staff Abraham Otieno.

Kenya’s advantage here is real: an educated, English-fluent workforce in a time zone that overlaps closely with US, UK, and European business hours. That lets us offer 24/7 support and overnight processing that feels local to your customers, not outsourced.

Today we work with 200+ businesses worldwide, helping them cut operating costs by 40-60% without a drop in service quality. Security isn’t an afterthought either: our facilities are ISO-compliant and GDPR-ready from day one.
